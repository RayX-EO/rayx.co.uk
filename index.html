01 / WHY IT MATTERS

Decisions are made months before the harvest is counted.

Official yield figures arrive long after the crop is cut, and satellite estimates rarely show how they were made. RayX can issue weekly from May — field by field, with intervals, and a record that can't quietly change.

2026 season · The 2024 UK final was published on 12 December 2024 — and official figures are themselves revised

What we're working towards

Every crop field measured, compared and audited from space — starting with wheat and maize.

One consistent, independent record of what land has produced, is producing and could produce — whoever farms it, and whatever records they keep.

05 / ABOUT RAYX

A decade of research, now live.

RayX is a UK agricultural intelligence company founded by Dr Feng Yin and developed from nearly a decade of Earth-observation research at UCL and the National Centre for Earth Observation (NCEO).

Built on satellite Earth observation, it gives an independent view of what land has produced, what it is likely to produce, where performance is persistently strong or weak, and how weather and management shape the outcome.

UCL · NCEO

Research heritage in Earth observation and crop science.

SIAC

Open-source atmospheric correction developed by our founder — peer-reviewed and assessed in the international ACIX-II intercomparison.

UKCEH · AgZero+
